# Music beds (licensed, not in git)

One instrumental cue per episode, named `<pillar>-<nn>.mp3` to match `music.cue` in the episode YAML
(e.g. `never-fell-01.mp3`, `tomorrow-morning-01.mp3`, `the-hinge-01.mp3`, `early-arrival-01.mp3`).
Source: Epidemic Sound or Artlist under the channel's licence. No AI-generated music in season one.
Moods per pillar are in `docs/06-voice-and-sound.md`. The assembler ducks the bed under the voice
and fades it out over the last 1.5 s.
