Hinge: *Japan Never Closes* (no sakoku edicts, 1635–39), *The Sarajevo Driver Takes the Right Road*, *Arkhipov Says Yes* (handled as consequence, never spectacle), *The Berlin Wall Stands*. Early Arrival: *Steam in Rome*, *Antibiotics in 1300* (with the honest caveat that penicillin is weak against plague; the episode is about wounds and childbirth, not the Black Death). Never Fell: *Ming Fleets Reach Lisbon*, *The Aztecs Survive*, *The Mongols Take Vienna*. Tomorrow Morning: *A Carrington Storm Hits Today*.
